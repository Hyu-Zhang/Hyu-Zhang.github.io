            <div class="col-12 about-main">
              <p>I am <strong>Haoyu Zhang</strong> (张昊宇), a Ph.D. candidate at the School of Computer Science and Technology, <a href="https://www.hitsz.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen)</a>, under the supervision of <a href="https://liqiangnie.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Prof. Liqiang Nie</a>, <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=o_DllmIAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Prof. Yaowei Wang</a>, and <a href="https://mengliu1991.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Prof. Meng Liu</a>. Prior to this, I received my M.S. degree from the School of Computer Science and Technology at <a href="https://www.sdu.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Shandong University</a>, where I was advised by Prof. Liqiang Nie and Prof. Meng Liu.</p>

              <p>My research interests include multimodal retrieval, egocentric vision, and spatial intelligence. To date, I have published more than 10 papers in leading CCF-A conferences and journals, including IEEE TPAMI, ACM TOIS, CVPR, ICML, NeurIPS, AAAI, and ACM MM. I have also achieved strong results in international CVPR challenges, securing 9 first-place finishes, 2 second-place finishes, and 3 third-place finishes.</p>
